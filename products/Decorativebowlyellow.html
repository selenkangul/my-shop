Care Instructions

To keep your handmade Jesmonite piece looking beautiful, gently clean it with a soft, damp cloth. Avoid using harsh cleaning products, chemicals or abrasive scrubbing materials, as these may damage the surface of the product.

Wipe away spills and strong-coloured substances, such as wine, coffee or tea, as soon as possible to help prevent staining.

For long-term colour preservation and maintenance, you can refresh the protective sealing of your product by applying a thin layer of beeswax once a year.

Please note that our products are intended for decorative purposes only. They are not suitable for food contact or consumption and should not be placed in areas exposed to frequent water contact.
