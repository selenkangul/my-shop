About Abagi

Abagi Art creates handmade, one-of-a-kind home decor and colorful earrings. Each piece brings a touch of beauty and character to your home and your personal style.

At Abagi Art, caring for nature is just as important as creating beautiful things. All products are made from Jesmonite, an eco-friendly and sustainable material.

Looking to discover a new hobby or create your own pieces? Abagi Art hosts friendly, creative group workshops. Just sign up and bring your creativity!
